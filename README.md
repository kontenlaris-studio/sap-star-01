A one-star review written in sap: the maple gave its sap for pancakes, then got blamed when the bottle leaked all over the kitchen table.

## Why this line

The tree is the reviewer, not the reviewed: it did the generous thing (sap for pancakes) and still took the blame for the mess — a one-star review from the victim of the spill, not the one who spilled it.
